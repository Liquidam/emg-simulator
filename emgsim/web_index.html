EMG Simulator — how to use

A simulated surface EMG sensor. Everything you see is computed live from a model of the body and sent out over OSC.

  1. Watch. A simulated player contracts at random, like a real person (scenario performer): rests, normal squeezes, accents, strong bursts, long holds, ramps, flurries. Each coloured lane is one muscle; gold = envelope, dashed = effort.
  2. Play it yourself. Hold SPACE anywhere on this page (or press the box in PERFORM) to contract. The big slider works too. Both add on top of the scenario.
  3. Shape it. PERFORM: activity (how often), intensity (how hard), scenario. MUSCLE: channels, motor units, fatigue. NOISE: electrode contact, hum, movement artifacts. Or try a Preset at the top (e.g. classroom_noisy, loose_electrode, fatigue_demo).
  4. Receive it (OSC OUT tab sets host, port and streams). Default: 127.0.0.1:9100
    /emgsim/raw ch1 ch2 …raw EMG in mV, 1000 messages/s
    /emgsim/env e1 e2 …envelope 0..1 (1 = maximal effort), 100/s
    /emgsim/features/Nrms mav wl zcr tkeo mnf mdf (switch on in OSC OUT)
    /bio, /adcdrop-in formats of the EMG6 ESP32 sensor and the WearPi
    In Max: [udpreceive 9100] → [route /emgsim/env] → [unpack f f f f].
  5. Control it from Max/Pd: send to port 9201, e.g. /emgsim/drive 0.7, /emgsim/mode pulses, /emgsim/set noise.contact 0.3.
  6. Record saves a .csv into the recordings folder. Quit stops the simulator.
